Frequently Asked Questions.

Answers about this preview storefront and how the shopping experience works.

Can I place a real order?

Not yet. This website is a portfolio/demo storefront and checkout is disabled.

Are the products and prices final?

No. Product names, pricing and availability are illustrative and can be replaced with real catalog data.

Does the shopping bag remember my selections?

Yes. The bag is saved locally in your browser on this device. No orders are submitted.

Are shipping and returns available?

No fulfillment service is connected. The shipping page is informational and should be updated before launch.

How can I contact the brand?

Use the contact page for available channels. No live support or contact form backend is configured.
