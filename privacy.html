Privacy Policy

How this demonstration site handles information.

Last updated: October 2026

Demo Storefront

This is a static demonstration website hosted on a platform such as GitHub Pages. It does not process purchases or collect payment details.

Local Storage

Your shopping bag selections are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. You can clear them by clearing this site's browser storage. This information is not sent to our servers by the site code.

Forms

Newsletter and contact forms are demonstrations only and do not transmit your submissions.

External Content

Product photographs load from Unsplash and fonts load from Google Fonts. Those providers may receive technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser requests their resources. Their own privacy policies apply.

Contact

Official privacy contact information will be published before the store begins operating.
